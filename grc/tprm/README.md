# TPRM deliverables (Phase 3)

Third-party and supply chain risk management for the fictional MediPay ([scenario](../scenario.md)). Written as if the CISO asked for help as a consultant.

| | Deliverable | One-line purpose |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | [Supplier inventory and tiering](a-supplier-inventory.md) | Who we depend on, and how much |
| (b) | [Due diligence questionnaire](b-due-diligence-questionnaire.md) | What we ask each tier before signing |
| (c) | [Contract and assurance checklist](c-contract-checklist.md) | What the contract must say |
| (d) | [Continuous monitoring approach](d-continuous-monitoring.md) | How we keep watching after signing |
| (e) | [Supplier compromise response and exit plan](e-response-and-exit.md) | What we do when a supplier is breached or fails |
| (f) | [One-page board brief](f-board-brief.md) | What the Board needs to know, with 5 measures |
| (g) | [Mapping table](g-mapping.md) | How it all lines up with the Pledge and the Bill |

**Status:** first drafts, written with AI help. The learner reviews each one. Regulatory points were checked against primary sources on 14 Nov 2026 (🔎), and links are given so they can be re-read.

**How the strands connect:** the questions in (b) about SBOMs, signing, pinned dependencies, scanning gates and OIDC are the ones I implemented myself in Days 2 to 7. So I know what a good answer looks like.
