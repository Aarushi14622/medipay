## What's uncertain (say this in interviews)

1. The Bill could change before Royal Assent, and supplier duties depend on regulations not yet written.
2. Whether a billing processor like MediPay would ever be designated is the regulator's judgement. Our view (D11) is "unlikely".
3. Mappings show **coverage**, not **compliance**. Nobody can be compliant with a law that doesn't exist yet.
